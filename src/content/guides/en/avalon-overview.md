> **Editorial draft — not a verified strategy guide.** The area and monster listings below were transcribed from in-game Field Info screenshots dated November 22, 2026. The reported rescue mechanic has not been reproduced on the current server. This draft is deliberately kept out of public guide listings.

## Avalon's three areas

Avalon has three areas. Their names were observed in the game's **Field → Nation → Avalon** interface.

| Area | Section name in Field Info | Current research coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | **Hall of Respite Entry** | Map and monster list observed |
| Second | **Hall of Respite** | Map and monster list observed; rescue encounter under investigation |
| Third | **Heart of Avalon** | Map observed; encounter details still need verification |

The third area's Field Info screenshot showed no monsters in the list. **An empty list does not establish that the area has no enemies or bosses.**

## Reported rescue encounter

A player has reported a special encounter on the second area: defeating certain monsters described as **“innocent”** can cause an additional enemy to appear.

This report is a useful lead, **not a confirmed rule for the current server**. In particular, we have not established:

- Which enemies count toward the trigger.
- Whether a fixed number of defeats is required.
- The identity or number of enemies summoned.
- Whether the behavior depends on dungeon state, objectives, or cooldowns.
- Whether the encounter behaves the same way in the current Atlantica Global version.

For now, do not treat this page as an instruction to defeat particular monsters or as a reliable spawn-timer guide.

## Heart of Avalon — third area

The third area is named **Heart of Avalon**. Its map is visible in Field Info, but the available screenshot does not identify its enemies or encounter objectives. This section remains a placeholder for separately verified observations.
