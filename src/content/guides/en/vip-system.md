## Overview

The new VIP system is similar to some mobile/browser games where a user can get benefits based on their VIP level.

It can be accessed from the following menu:

![image.png](/images/guides/vip-system/vip-menu.png)

## How It Works

The system is account-wide, so you don't need to level up your VIP with multiple characters.

Experience is earned by expending GC. All players are also entitled to 22 free experience daily:

![image.png](/images/guides/vip-system/image-2.png)

**Based on your VIP level**, you can get the following benefits:

- Access to the Aca VIP Shop
- Instant diary completion (both for normal and ALVL diaries)
- Extra stuff for Olympus Tower on their HOT days
- Access to Buff Item Auto-Consume Slots
- Access to Atlantica VIP Material Bag 

Below is a table with information regarding the experience and GC required to reach all VIP levels, as well how many days/years it'll take for F2P players to achieve the same levels:

![image.png](/images/guides/vip-system/image-3.png)

As you can see, at some point it becomes pretty much Whale territory, so plan your progression accordingly.
