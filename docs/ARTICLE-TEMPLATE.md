
# Article Template

Standard article structure for the Atlantica Knowledge Hub.

Use this template when creating guides through Pages CMS.

Article metadata (title, category, subcategory, language,
description, updated date, and draft status) must be completed
using the CMS fields.

---

## Overview

Briefly explain the subject of the article and its purpose.

## Requirements

List any prerequisites, including:

- Character level or other requirements
- Required items or equipment
- Access conditions
- Relevant game mechanics

Remove this section when not applicable.

## How It Works

Explain the mechanics, rules, or systems involved.

Break complex explanations into smaller subsections.

### Important Details

Describe relevant values, restrictions, or interactions.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Describe the first step.
2. Describe the next step.
3. Continue until the procedure is complete.

Use screenshots or GIFs when they improve clarity.

## Tips and Recommendations

Provide useful strategies, optimizations, or warnings.

## Common Problems

Describe common mistakes and possible solutions.

## Additional Information

Include supplementary information or related mechanics.

## References

List relevant sources, official announcements,
or links supporting the information.
